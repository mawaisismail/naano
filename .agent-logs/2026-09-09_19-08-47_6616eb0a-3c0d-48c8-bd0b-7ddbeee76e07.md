## The business

Naano sells **borrowed trust, priced per post.**

A B2B brand wants pipeline. LinkedIn Ads gets them impressions people scroll past. Meanwhile, a RevOps practitioner with 12K followers writes a post that their exact buyers actually read and believe. Naano makes that second thing buyable — like ad inventory, but the inventory is credibility.

Two sides:
- **Creators** list themselves at a flat fee per post they set, from €20.
- **Brands** browse, book (single or bulk), send a brief, and get a post carrying a tracked link.

Naano sits in the middle running the brief, the contract, the invoice and the payout, so neither side handles the other's admin.

## The numbers

From their own published data — median per post: **€84** under 5K followers, **€180** at 5–10K, **€312** at 10–25K, up to **€400–1,500** for top creators. Roughly 2,000–3,000 vetted creators across 100 countries.

The marketplace itself is easy to clone — we just cloned it in a day.

## Where it's weak

1. **Disintermediation.** A brand books a creator once, likes them, then DMs them directly next time. Classic marketplace leak. Payments and attribution are the only things holding them in.

3. **Creator pricing is arbitrary.** Creators set their own fee with no feedback from performance. A creator driving €12 leads and one driving €90 leads can charge the same.
4. **Vetting doesn't scale.** "Vetted" is a human bottleneck at 3,000 creators and a liability at 30,000.
